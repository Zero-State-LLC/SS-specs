# D-114 fixture review

D-114 changes how an aware standard enemy steers when a solid blocks its line to the Player. It also gates the Correlator charge and the Witch cast on a clear line.

None of the golden fixtures exercises this path:
- The complete-run vectors reach their end states through test helpers (`testing_completeRunSuccess`, seed 1). No enemy steers there.
- The smoke replay is five ticks long, before any encounter starts.

So their canonical end states are unchanged except for `rulesetVersion`.

## Method

1. On runtime `3d3a0ac` (ruleset-010), the four canonical end states were dumped with `StateDigest.canonical(...).serialize()`:
   - the three complete-run vectors;
   - the smoke replay (`replay-smoke-001` through `Simulation.run`).

   All four hashed to the stored ruleset-010 digests.
2. In each dump exactly one field was replaced, `"rulesetVersion":"ss-rules-010"` → `"ss-rules-011"`, and the result was hashed with SHA-256. Those hashes are the new expectations below. No failing test output was copied to produce them.
3. The ruleset-011 runtime must reproduce them by actual execution, in the full suite.

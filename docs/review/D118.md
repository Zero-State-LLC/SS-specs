# D-118 fixture review

D-118 changes two things:
- the Captain's steering when a solid blocks it;
- the Captain's base speed, from 120 to 96.

None of the golden fixtures runs a Captain fight:
- The complete-run vectors reach their end states through test helpers (`testing_completeRunSuccess`, seed 1).
- The smoke replay is five ticks long.

So their canonical end states are unchanged except for `rulesetVersion` and `contentVersion`.

## Method

1. On runtime `cb56a28` (ruleset-013, content-008, SS-runtime#163), the four canonical end states were dumped with `StateDigest.canonical(...).serialize()`:
   - the three complete-run vectors;
   - the smoke replay.

   All four hashed to the stored ruleset-013 digests.
2. In each dump exactly two fields were replaced, and the result was hashed with SHA-256:
   - `"rulesetVersion":"ss-rules-013"` → `"ss-rules-014"`;
   - `"contentVersion":"civic-seam-content-008"` → `"civic-seam-content-009"`.

   Those hashes are the new expectations below. No failing test output was copied to produce them.
3. The ruleset-014 runtime reproduces them by actual execution: the full Linux suite passed, 842 tests.

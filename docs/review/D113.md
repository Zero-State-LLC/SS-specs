# D-113 fixture review

D-113 changes two rules:
- Ricochet continues only from a Civic Pulse hit that defeats its enemy target;
- with Signal Jammer, each M-A/M-B heat reinforcement is 1 lower.

Neither applies along the golden fixture paths:
- The complete-run vectors reach their end states through test helpers (`testing_completeRunSuccess`, seed 1). They fire no Civic Pulse at an enemy, and start no wave with a heat reinforcement.
- The smoke replay is five ticks long.

So their canonical end states are unchanged except for `rulesetVersion` and `contentVersion`.

## Method

1. On runtime `747b086` (ruleset-009, content-006), the four canonical end states were dumped with `StateDigest.canonical(...).serialize()`:
   - the three complete-run vectors;
   - the smoke replay (`replay-smoke-001` through `Simulation.run`).

   All four hashed to the stored ruleset-009 digests.
2. In each dump exactly two fields were replaced, and the result was hashed with SHA-256:
   - `"rulesetVersion":"ss-rules-009"` → `"ss-rules-010"`;
   - `"contentVersion":"civic-seam-content-006"` → `"civic-seam-content-007"`.

   Those hashes are the new expectations below. No failing test output was copied to produce them.
3. The ruleset-010 runtime must reproduce them by actual execution, in the full suite.

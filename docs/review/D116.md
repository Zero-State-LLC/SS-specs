- The complete-run vectors reach their end states through test helpers (`testing_completeRunSuccess`, seed 1).
- The smoke replay is five ticks long, before any encounter starts.

So their canonical end states are unchanged except for `rulesetVersion`.

## Method

1. On the D-115 runtime (ruleset-012, content-008), the four canonical end states were dumped with `StateDigest.canonical(...).serialize()`:
   - the three complete-run vectors;
   - the smoke replay.

   All four hashed to the stored ruleset-012 digests.
2. In each dump exactly one field was replaced, `"rulesetVersion":"ss-rules-012"` → `"ss-rules-013"`, and the result was hashed with SHA-256. Those hashes are the new expectations below. No failing test output was copied to produce them.
3. The ruleset-013 runtime must reproduce them by actual execution, in the full suite.
